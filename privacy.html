Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Clutch in Vice Studio ("the app") is an internal tool used only by Clutch in Vice to publish its own videos to its own social media accounts.

What the app accesses

When we connect our own YouTube, TikTok or other accounts, the app receives access tokens from those platforms. It uses them only to upload our videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read the status and basic statistics of our own uploads.

What we store

Access tokens and our own video files are stored only on our own computer. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about viewers or any other users.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok

TikTok data is handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy; access can be revoked in TikTok's app settings.

Deletion

Revoking access removes the app's ability to use the account. To have stored tokens deleted, email us.
